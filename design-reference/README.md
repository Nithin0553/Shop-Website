# Approved visual references

These three WebP screenshots are compressed copies of the initial approved UI/UX mockups. They are **design references**, not page backgrounds or production webpage screenshots.

- `homepage.webp` — navigation, product photography hero, brands, category tiles, reasons to choose us, latest blogs, dark-blue contact bar, footer.
- `blogs.webp` — featured article, sidebar category filter, six article cards, newsletter block, contact bar, footer.
- `about-contact.webp` — shop story, storefront illustration, trust cards, brands, visit/store map area and enquiry form.

The mockups contain illustrative shop imagery and fictional phone, address, date, and hours examples. **Do not copy those into the production website.** Enter real business details using the Admin dashboard.

The implementation follows their composition and color system, while using real functionality, responsive layouts, accessible controls, and safe placeholders for missing business data.
