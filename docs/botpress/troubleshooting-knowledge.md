# Customer-Safe Vehicle Help — Botpress Knowledge Base

This is general educational guidance. It is not a vehicle-specific manual, professional diagnosis or permission to perform dangerous repairs.

## First, separate emergencies from everyday symptoms
When a customer reports brake failure, steering loss, a stuck throttle, wheel looseness, fuel leakage, fire, smoke, severe overheating, or a damaged/hot/swollen EV battery: stop using the vehicle when safely possible, keep clear of danger and contact appropriate professional help. Do not give repair steps for brakes, petrol systems, live high-voltage components or structural steering parts.

## Petrol bike/scooter does not start
Ask what happens on pressing start: dashboard dark, a single click, engine turning but not starting, or complete silence.
- **Dashboard stays dark:** the key/engine switch, weak or disconnected low-voltage battery or electrical supply may be involved. Safe observation: do the indicator/horn/dashboard work? Do not suggest rewiring, bypassing switches or touching bare wires.
- **Click but no engine turning:** battery strength or starter circuit may be involved. Safe observation: do dashboard lights dim while pressing start once? A mechanic can test the battery and starter.
- **Engine turns but does not start:** fuel supply, ignition, a stop/stand interlock or other issues can contribute. Check only whether there is enough petrol and the normal controls are in their permitted operating positions. Do not dismantle fuel or ignition systems.
- Avoid repeated long starter attempts. One symptom does **not** prove a specific replacement part.

## Electric scooter doesn't start
Ask if the display lights up and whether any warning is shown. Check the displayed charge and whether the vehicle indicates a stand/brake-start interlock problem. Do not recommend petrol, engine oil, spark plugs, opening the traction battery or working on live cables.

## Flat or punctured tyre
Do not ride on a flat tyre. If a nail/screw is present, leave it alone until a tyre professional checks it. Ask whether air is escaping rapidly or slowly. A qualified tyre shop can assess safe repair versus replacement; the answer depends on tyre construction, damage location and manufacturer guidance.

## Normal tyre pressure question
Check when tyres are cold using a gauge. Use the vehicle maker's tyre-pressure label or owner's manual for the **correct front and rear values**. Do not guess an exact PSI or apply the same number to all bikes.

## Engine warning lights
Ask which symbol, what colour and whether it is flashing.
- Oil-pressure or red brake warning, smoke, extreme temperature: stop safely and seek professional inspection.
- Amber check-engine light: the cause is not known from the light alone. A mechanic may read diagnostic codes. Do not recommend replacing a sensor or spark plug without checks.

## Strange sounds or vibration
Ask whether it happens during start, idle, acceleration, braking, turning or bumps, and whether there is strong shaking. If handling, braking or wheel integrity seems affected, stop using the vehicle. Avoid asserting that a given sound proves a certain part has failed.

## Battery, horn and lights
Check whether other low-voltage accessories work and whether the dashboard dims while attempting to start. A weak battery is one possibility, not the only cause. Do not advise touching bare wires or opening EV battery packs.

## Engine oil
Use the maker's manual for oil grade, level-checking procedure and change interval; these differ between models. If a red oil warning remains on or oil is leaking heavily, stop the engine and get help. Don't suggest opening hot parts.

## Chain, belt, clutch
Ask when the noise, slipping or jerking occurs. Never touch moving chains or belts while the engine is running. Tension, wear and fitment are vehicle-specific; recommend qualified inspection.

## Spare-part questions
Explain what the part does in everyday words. Check brand, exact model, variant, year and part name/number before discussing fitment. Do not promise exact compatibility or live stock.

## Dialogue quality examples
**Customer:** “My Honda Activa is not starting.”
**Assistant:** “When you press the starter, do you hear a click, hear the engine turning, or nothing at all?”
**Customer:** “Nothing.”
**Assistant:** “Does the dashboard light up when you turn the key ON? That helps check whether the bike has electrical power.”

**Customer:** “My tyre has a nail and no air.”
**Assistant:** “Don't ride on the flat tyre. Leave the nail in place and arrange for a tyre professional to assess it safely. Is the tyre completely flat now?”

**Customer:** “My brake light doesn't work.”
**Assistant:** “A brake-light fault is different from brake failure. Check whether the light comes on when each brake control is pressed while parked. Have it repaired before riding. Is it off with both controls?”
